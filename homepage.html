Questions teams ask before they start

What calls does Callmetrics track?

Incoming, outgoing, missed and rejected calls, with duration, ringing time, unique clients and the agent who handled each one.

Can I keep office numbers out of my reports?

Yes. Add them to the excluded numbers list. They are left out of dashboard figures and reports, but the call logs are still saved.

Can I listen to recordings?

Yes. Open the recordings library, search or filter, then play, download or delete a recording. You can also turn on auto delete for old recordings.

Can I see where my agents are?

Yes. The live agent dashboard shows each agent’s last known location on a map and whether they are available, on a call or on field.

Can I export my reports?

Yes. Print the analytics report as a PDF and export call history to Excel.

How many agents can I add?

It depends on your plan. The billing page shows your agent limit and how many you are using.
